# Global legal and economic knowledge layer

## Included case-law starter digests

The bundled retrieval index contains educational summaries for:

- United States: *Marbury v. Madison*, *Brown v. Board of Education*, *Miranda v. Arizona*, *Obergefell v. Hodges*.
- United Kingdom: *Donoghue v. Stevenson*.
- Canada: *R v. Oakes*.
- Australia: *Mabo v Queensland (No 2)* (High Court of Australia).
- India: *Kesavananda Bharati*, *Maneka Gandhi*, *Vishaka*, *K.S. Puttaswamy* (Supreme Court of India), and *Naz Foundation v. Government of NCT of Delhi* (Delhi High Court), with its subsequent appellate history flagged.
- South Africa: *S v. Makwanyane* and *Grootboom*.
- European Union: *Van Gend en Loos*.
- European human-rights system: *Handyside v. United Kingdom*.

These are compact secondary digests, not full judgments. The URL metadata points to the relevant court or case-law repository, not necessarily to a deep link for the individual decision. Retrieve the exact judgment and later treatment before relying on any proposition. The database does not claim exhaustive coverage of any court.

## Economic change and shortage domains

The starter index includes frameworks for macroeconomic indicators, food/agriculture and food-price risk, energy and critical materials, and trade/logistics/supply-chain disruption. It does not contain live market readings or assert that a shortage is currently occurring.

The official-source directory includes World Bank, IMF, FAO, WFP, IEA, WTO, UNCTAD and BIS sources. For each current claim, capture geography, observation period, publication/release date, unit, data vintage/revision, source URL and whether the value is observed, estimated or forecast. A price increase alone is not proof of a physical shortage.

## Updating the library

Use the in-app Knowledge Library to upload public/licensed text-based PDFs, Markdown or text documents and provide the correct jurisdiction, authority, language, as-of date and source URL. Scanned PDFs need OCR before upload. Do not upload confidential or privileged case material without appropriate legal authority and safeguards.
