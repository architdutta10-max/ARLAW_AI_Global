# ARLAW AI Global 3.1.1 — Legal coverage update

## Scope added

The bundled legal retrieval corpus now includes 15 topic/jurisdiction digests plus four global-comparison corporate-liability records, covering:

1. India Constitution Article 21, *Maneka Gandhi* and privacy doctrine.
2. Privacy/data protection: India (DPDP Act and phased commencement), Germany/EU (GDPR and BDSG), UK (UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018), and US (federal sectoral and state frameworks).
3. Corporate fraud, director duties and personal/entity liability: India, UK, US and Germany, with separate source-backed global-comparison retrieval.
4. Cartels: Competition Act 2002 (India) and Article 101 TFEU / Regulation 1/2003 (EU).
5. Trade controls: India SCOMET/DGFT, Germany/EU dual-use controls and sanctions, Singapore Strategic Goods (Control) Act, and UN sanctions context.

## Retrieval and safety notes

- Legal retrieval remains jurisdiction constrained. Domestic legal sources are not silently mixed across jurisdictions.
- Broad corporate-liability questions retrieve separately labelled comparison digests; the response must still identify jurisdiction-specific limits.
- The India DPDP entry records phased commencement under Gazette notification G.S.R. 843(E) of 13 November 2025. As of 25 November 2026, the one-year provisions have commenced; the main substantive provisions are scheduled for 13 May 2027 unless changed by a later instrument. Always verify the latest Gazette.
- The US privacy entry expressly avoids claiming there is one comprehensive federal privacy statute.
- The Singapore entry distinguishes Singapore's implementation of UN sanctions from unilateral foreign sanctions and notes that separate foreign-law nexus can still matter.
- Curated digests are secondary educational summaries linked to primary materials. They are not full official statute text, not a substitute for current consolidated legislation, and do not independently verify currency or legal advice.
