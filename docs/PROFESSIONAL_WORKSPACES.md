# ARLAW AI Global — Professional Workspaces

ARLAW provides workflow and research assistance across legal, accounting, tax and judicial-research tasks. It is not a replacement for licensed or regulated professionals, courts, or public authorities.

## Workspaces

- **Lawyer & advocate assistance:** issue spotting, fact organisation, research plans, working drafts and questions for counsel. A qualified advocate must review legal strategy and filings.
- **Accounting support:** bookkeeping explanations, journal-entry reasoning, reconciliation workflows and financial-statement analysis. It does not certify accounts or perform statutory audits.
- **CA & tax support:** jurisdiction- and tax-year-specific research, worked calculations and compliance preparation. Current rates, forms, deadlines and rules must be checked against official sources. It cannot sign, attest, certify or conduct regulated audits.
- **Judicial research support:** neutral organisation of procedural history, party submissions, evidence references and precedent comparisons. It must not decide guilt, liability, credibility or outcomes.
- **Legal research:** identify jurisdiction, authority hierarchy, effective date, legal issue and primary sources.

## Reliability controls

1. State jurisdiction, relevant date, tax year or reporting period where applicable.
2. Prefer current official primary sources and record URL, authority, language and as-of date when indexing.
3. Separate source text, inference, assumptions, disputed facts and professional opinion.
4. Never invent statutes, cases, tax rates, deadlines, accounting standards or citations.
5. Citation validation currently verifies only that a cited `[S#]` label exists in the retrieved set. It does not establish that the source supports the proposition or is current.
6. Multi-model review is an additional opinion, not independent proof or a guarantee of correctness.
7. Require qualified human review before filing, signing, certifying, auditing, submitting tax returns or relying on high-impact conclusions.
8. Do not upload privileged, confidential, personal or financial records unless there is an appropriate legal basis, consent, access control and security process.

## Current implementation boundary

Task routing selects specialist instructions based on the user's message. It does not create independently trained professional models, verified calculation engines, a complete legal/accounting database, professional accreditation or regulatory authority. The quality of answers depends on configured model providers and the sources indexed by the operator.
