# Global readiness roadmap

Included now: global jurisdiction selector, multi-jurisdiction metadata, local source retrieval,
official portal directory starting points, local inference, document upload.

Required before professional deployment:
- Per-country official gazette, legislation, court and regulator connectors where permitted.
- Versioned statutes and amendment/supersession tracking.
- State/province/court-level jurisdiction hierarchy.
- Multilingual embeddings, OCR and cross-language retrieval.
- Exact quotation and pinpoint citation verification.
- Separate workflows for law, tax, accounting, economics and justice.
- Benchmarks by jurisdiction and language, including temporal legal questions.
- Expert review, feedback provenance and correction workflow.
- Authentication, tenant isolation, encryption, audit logs, backups, rate limits and security testing.

Never claim comprehensive global coverage, professional status, or guaranteed legal correctness.
