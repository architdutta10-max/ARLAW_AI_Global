# ARLAW AI Global 3.1.3

- Merges the 3.1.1 evidence-gated legal corpus with 3.1.2 selective local chat deletion and reliability safeguards.
- Preserves the 3.1.2 task-routing, managed knowledge refresh and no-source safeguards.
- Allocates retrieval slots across jurisdictions for global comparisons instead of allowing one jurisdiction to consume all source slots.
- Raises the default retrieved-context budget to 10,000 characters (still configurable, capped at 16,000).
- Adds EU-jurisdiction GDPR and Dual-Use Regulation research digests linked to EUR-Lex primary texts.
- Replaces the long generic reliability banner with a concise source-check notice.
- This release is not a certification of legal accuracy, completeness, or current validity.
